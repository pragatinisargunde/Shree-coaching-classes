Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about admissions and hostel life

❓ What are the admission requirements?

We require previous academic report cards, student identification, passport-size photographs, and parent/guardian contact verification. Special scholarship test registrations are held separately.

❓ Are hostel facilities mandatory for enrolled students?

No, hostel accommodation is optional. Day scholar batches are also available. However, for outstation students, our campus hostel offers dedicated evening study sessions and zero daily commute.

❓ What payment methods and installment options are available?

We accept bank wire transfers, UPI / QR payments, and debit/credit cards. Flexible installment-based structures are available through the finance desk upon request.
