Frequently Asked Questions

Quickly search and find answers regarding admissions, hostel timings, and mess facilities.

What are the hostel gate timings for students? ▼

The main hostel gate opens at 06:00 AM and closes strictly at 09:30 PM everyday for student safety and discipline.

Are parents allowed to visit students on weekdays? ▼

Yes, guardian visiting hours are Monday to Saturday from 04:30 PM to 07:00 PM, and Sundays from 09:00 AM to 06:00 PM.

You can view the day-wise 4-meal diet schedule directly on our Hostel & Mess page using the interactive Mon-Sun tabs.
