Our Story

Founded in 1999, EduHostel Academy has been at the forefront of providing exceptional education combined with comfortable residential living facilities.

Over the past 25 years, we celebrate major cultural & national festivals like Ganpati Aagman & Visarjan with Lezim-Dhol, Shivjayanti, Gurupurnima, Rakshabandhan, Holi, Makar Sankranti, and host regular sports, weekly tests, and Sarav Pariksha.

"Education is not merely the loading of youth minds with knowledge, it is about igniting their inner potential to build a brighter future."

Welcome to EduHostel Academy. Since our inception in 1999, our core objective has been to blend rigorous scholastic training with a secure, disciplined, and nurturing residential environment.

We believe every student possesses unique capabilities. Through our specialized scholarship batches, personal mentorship, and structured daily routines, we ensure that students develop both academic brilliance and high moral character.
